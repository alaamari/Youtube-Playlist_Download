Privacy & acceptable use

Updated 19 November 2026

This website

This is a static website. It does not accept playlist links, run a download API, or process media. It serves the app information and installer links. Vercel may process standard website request information such as IP addresses, timestamps and user agents. GitHub serves installer downloads under its own privacy policy.

The Mac app

The Mac app processes downloads on your computer and saves them to the folder you choose. It contacts Spotify to read track information and public media providers to find matching audio. Those providers receive your IP address and requests under their own policies. Spotify links provide metadata; the app does not download protected Spotify audio. App preferences and temporary tool data are stored on your Mac.

Use only with permission

Download only media you own or have explicit permission to save. Follow source-platform terms and applicable law. A public listing or playable song does not grant download rights. Matching may select a different recording; review the results.

Support

Report security concerns, privacy questions or removal requests through the project issue tracker. Never post passwords, private signing keys, browser cookies or other secrets in an issue.

Open source notices

Application source, build instructions and third-party notices are included in the project repository and packaged app. Check the release notes before installing.

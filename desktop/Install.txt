YouTube & Spotify Downloader 1.3.0 for Mac

1. Open the disk image and drag the app onto Applications.
2. Open the app from Applications. Paste a YouTube or Spotify link.
3. Choose your folder and click Download. Songs save one at a time.

Everything needed for downloads is included. No Python, Homebrew, Terminal setup, cookies.txt, or access key is required. Internet access is needed when downloading.

This independent build is not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks the first launch, review the app's source and release page, then use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway if you trust it. Do not disable macOS security settings. Official guidance: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

Choose Apple Silicon for Macs with M1, M2, M3, M4, M5 or later Apple chips. Choose Intel for Intel Macs. macOS 12 or later is required.

Spotify links supply metadata. Audio is matched on Bandcamp, then YouTube, and may differ from Spotify's recording. Source availability and restrictions can vary. Conversion does not improve the source quality.

Only download media you have permission to save.

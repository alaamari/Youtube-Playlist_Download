Privacy & acceptable use

Updated 24 October 2026

Downloads on your Mac

The Mac app saves media to the folder you choose and stores its preferences and temporary tool data on your Mac. It contacts Spotify to read track information and media providers to find and download matching audio. Those services receive your IP address and requests under their own policies. Spotify links supply metadata; this app does not download protected Spotify audio.

This website

Public cloud processing is currently disabled. This website does not accept account passwords, browser cookies, media uploads or download jobs. Vercel serves this website and may process standard request logs such as IP addresses, timestamps and user agents. GitHub serves installer downloads under its own privacy policy. We do not include advertising or analytics scripts in this website.

Use only with permission

Download only media you own or have explicit permission to download. Follow the source platform’s terms and applicable law. A Spotify listing, a publicly playable song or this app’s availability does not grant download rights. Do not use this app to bypass access controls or copy protected media without authorization. Matching may select a different recording; review the results.

Support and removal requests

Report security concerns, privacy questions or removal requests through the project’s issue tracker. Include the affected public URL and a description. Never post passwords, private signing keys, browser cookies or other secrets in an issue.

Open source notices

Application source, build instructions and third-party notices are included in the project repository and packaged app. Check the release notes before installing.
